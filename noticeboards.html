Public Bridge

For people seeking connection after self-reflection. This page turns only chosen, permissioned pieces of private Markdown into a low-pressure commons signal, so saying hello feels easier and less exposed.

Selective means not everything travels. The bridge carries only what someone chooses to share, at the visibility they choose. It is not a profile dump, confession or permission to infer more.
