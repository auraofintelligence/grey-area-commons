Ease first

The first job is to lower pressure. Questions should help people feel more honest, warm, grounded and free to pause.

Mutual yes

Connection becomes easier when people can name what feels welcome, what needs time, and what only belongs inside clear mutual interest.

Owned by you

The forms create `.md` files in the browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored on a server, or made public by this static site.

The Conversation

This site sits between public identity, private aura, loving curiosity and real-world connection. It is for the tender middle where people need better questions before they need labels, promises, introductions or posts.

The goal is to make closeness feel less like a leap and more like a path: ease first, warmth next, then consent, timing, distance, diversity, AI help and public context only where they belong.
