Memory, Love and Intimacy

A softer form for adults navigating dementia onset, family history of memory-related decline, diagnosis, care or recovery hope. It asks how tenderness, attraction, touch, privacy and consent can stay human when memory or capacity may change.

Care guardrail. This form does not diagnose, treat, replace a doctor, decide consent or manage emergencies. It helps people prepare calmer words for loved ones, carers, clinicians, counsellors and trusted support people.
